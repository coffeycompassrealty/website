Why it's different

Buyers ask different questions.

Land buyers want to know about zoning, utilities, septic, road frontage, survey lines and what they can build. We get those answers lined up before we list, so a buyer can say yes faster.

  1. Know what you have

    Survey, zoning, access, utilities and soil. We find out what's there and what's possible.

  2. Price it like land, not a house

    Land values move on different things than homes do. We price off land sales, not house comps.

  3. Split it or sell it whole

    Sometimes a parcel is worth more as lots. Mark's construction background helps us run those numbers with you.

  4. Reach builders and buyers

    Drone photos, clear maps and our list of builders and land buyers who are already looking.
