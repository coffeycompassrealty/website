About this property

You'll recognize the Belleville II is a modern, well-designed home as soon as you walk inside. The flex room off the foyer is a personalized space, designed to fit your lifestyle. Use it as a home office for work from home, play room for the kids, or relaxing library. The floor plan also includes a pocket office just off the family foyer. All the most desired elements of today's home design can be found in the great room featuring a high-ceiling family room with a corner fireplace, an open kitchen with an island/breakfast bar leading into the dining area, and the option of a sunroom to bring the outdoors in. Upstairs you'll find four bedrooms, each with generous closet space and some with private bath options, as well as a convenient upstairs laundry room. You can also customize this floorplan with a game room, all depending on your family's needs. The rest of the upstairs is taken up by the luxurious primary suite with its huge walk-in closet and spa-like bath. Build your dream custom home with the Belleville II floor plan on 7.67 incredible acres in Morgan County!
